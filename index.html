About the programme

What this 20-day engagement actually delivers

TISSIR 2025 is not a slide deck. It is a working weekly schedule that lands inside your factory and walks the team from a written quality manual to a floor that the buyer will accept on audit day. Every session has a clear topic, a clear day, and a clear takeaway the team has to evidence by the next morning.

Programme goal

Move your facility from "we have the manual" to "we can show the buyer, on the spot, what we do and how we record it." Every session is designed to create evidence the auditor can verify.

Who is on the floor

Compliance officers, quality managers, production leaders, supervisors, line operators, warehouse, HR, and the management team who will host the auditor. Sessions are mixed-language and shop-floor friendly.

Learning philosophy

Short evening sessions keep pace manageable. Two full mock-audit days simulate the real thing. Every coaching topic is tied to a control area the buyer will open — so the team never trains on something "abstract".

Coaching pillars

  • Quality manual understanding and process ownership
  • Document and records discipline, in the language the team speaks
  • Audit evidence generation — not just theory
  • On-floor quality and safety habits that survive the audit

What each session looks like

  • Topic walk-through with real factory documents
  • Practical exercise on the actual line / area
  • Live evidence (logs, registers, photos) created during the session
  • Open questions parked for the next morning briefing

What you walk away with

  • A controlled document, register, or log filed by your own team
  • An action list assigned to a named owner with a date
  • A clearer picture of where the audit risk sits, session by session
  • Two days of full mock-audit experience before the real one
Standards & buyer expectations

The reference points the team will hear through the engagement

These standards cards are used as a guided introduction before the detail work begins. They are referenced again during Phase D and during the mock audit.

ISO 9001

Quality policy, document control, risk thinking, internal audit, CAPA, and management-review discipline. Frame for every QMS conversation.

Buyer Technical Audit

Factory capability, production controls, in-process quality, traceability, calibration, contamination prevention, and final-inspection readiness. The core of the engagement.

SMETA & Social

Worker protection, HR documentation, training records, accountability, emergency preparedness, and respectful workplace controls. Addressed in S14 and FD-1/FD-2.

Environmental

Chemical inventory, SDS management, storage, spill readiness, waste discipline, and environmental monitoring logs. Treated in S6, S14, and the chemical control checklist.

OCS · Organic Content Standard

Material integrity, label accuracy, contamination prevention, traceability, and documented handling procedures for organic-content claims. Used wherever the buyer's brief calls for verified organic-content flow.

Customer Requirements

Buyer-specific tolerances, AQL rules (F-18), inspection forms, PP meetings, sample approvals, and reporting expectations. Calibrated to your actual buyer during Phase B.

How the training runs

The weekly rhythm, the working pattern, and what each day looks like

This is what the team will actually experience — a structured rhythm of evening classroom sessions plus two full-day mock audits, with a fixed working window and a fixed coach.

Weekly working pattern

The engagement runs Monday to Saturday for three consecutive weeks. Sundays are off by design — your team rests, the coach reviews the closed-loop actions from the week.

  • Week 1 (03/08 → 09/08): Phase A & start of Phase B — QMS foundation and material control.
  • Week 2 (10/08 → 16/08): Phase B end + Phase C — production-floor cluster.
  • Week 3 (17/08 → 22/08): Phase D records + Phase E mock audit & handover.

Evening sessions (S1 → S15 + S16)

Sixteen evening sessions, three hours each (17h15 → 20h00). They run after the production shift so line leaders can attend without leaving the floor during the day. Topics follow the 5-phase calendar.

  • 17h15 → 18h00 — topic walk-through & document inspection
  • 18h00 → 19h15 — practical exercise on the floor / area
  • 19h15 → 20h00 — evidence creation, owner assignment, Q&A

Full mock-audit days (FD-1 + FD-2)

Two intensive full-day simulations, 08h00 → 17h00, with breaks. They are scheduled just before the closing debrief so the team has a final, real-time rehearsal of what the buyer will do.

  • FD-1 · Thursday 20/08: mock audit on the production floor.
  • FD-2 · Friday 21/08: mock audit document review & scoring.
  • S16 · Saturday 22/08: closing debrief, action owners, handover.
